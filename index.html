About the Ride

Tour De Pints is Seattle's self-paced cycling brewery crawl — a free, social ride through the Emerald City's most beloved neighborhoods, with stops at some of the finest craft breweries in the Pacific Northwest.

From Fremont to Georgetown, Ballard to Capitol Hill, each route tells a different story of the city. No race timers, no entry fees, no judgment — just good people, good bikes, and the kind of beer you can only find in Seattle.

"Fueled by coffee. Rewarded with craft beer. Washed down by Seattle rain."

Participants ride at their own pace and are responsible for following traffic laws, wearing helmets, and drinking responsibly. This is a public road ride, not a race.

Support the Ride

Every ride is completely free to join. If you'd like to help cover route permits, event supplies, and keep future rides rolling, any donation is deeply appreciated.

Donations are not tax-deductible. Every dollar goes directly to event costs.
