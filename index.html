About the Tour

Founded in 2011, The Tour De Pints are semi-guided group rides for riders of all skill levels. Originally just a single ride, there are now multiple Seattle tours for different areas! The routes are different every year, but always aim to stick to separated bike paths, bike lanes, and bike friendly streets. Along the way we stop at local breweries, especially bike themed ones, for beers and swag raffles, and we make friends along the way.

Homegrown in Seattle, the ride is organized by volunteers and supported by local breweries. There are different events for different parts of town, including West Seattle, North Seattle, and the East Side. If you're interested in bringing a ride to your neighborhood, or want to help out with organizing, shoot us an email or join the Discord!

Support the Ride

Every ride is completely free to join. If you'd like to help cover event supplies, and keep future rides rolling, any donation is appreciated.

Donations are not tax-deductible. Every dollar goes directly to event costs and growing future events.
